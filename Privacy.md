# UToolkit Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: July 12, 2026

生效日期: 2026 年 7 月 12 日

## English

UToolkit does not require an account and does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or upload personal data or user content to developer-operated servers.

Most features process data locally on the device. This includes text and Markdown entered into conversion and developer tools, passwords generated by the password tool, cryptographic inputs and outputs, QR code images, device diagnostics, and serial data. Password history exists only in memory while the password tool is in use and is not persisted to disk.

Network tools communicate directly from the user's device with local or remote targets selected by the user. Port scanning, LAN scanning, Ping, and Traceroute run only after user action. These connections are not routed through developer-operated servers. Users are responsible for scanning only systems and networks they are authorized to test.

Camera, photo library, motion sensor, local network, and USB access are requested only where a feature needs them and the operating system supports them. QR code scanning and generation, document export, motion diagnostics, local-network discovery, and serial communication operate only when initiated by the user. Serial data stays between the user's device and connected hardware and is not uploaded to developer-operated servers.

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. UToolkit reads Apple-provided product and entitlement status to unlock Pro features; it does not receive or store payment-card details. Apple's handling of App Store purchases and diagnostics is governed by Apple's privacy policy and the user's device and App Store settings.

Apple may provide crash or performance reports according to the user's Apple diagnostics settings. The developer may use those reports only to diagnose and improve UToolkit and retains them only as long as needed for that purpose.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue in the [UToolKit GitHub repository](https://github.com/lslqtz/UToolKit/issues). Do not include passwords, cryptographic keys, serial logs, documents, or other sensitive content in a public issue.

## 简体中文

UToolkit 不要求用户注册账户，不包含广告或由开发者运营的分析服务。开发者不会收集、出售、出租个人数据，也不会将个人数据或用户内容上传至开发者运营的服务器。

大部分功能都在设备本地处理数据，包括用户输入到转换和开发工具中的文本与 Markdown、密码工具生成的密码、加密输入与输出、二维码图片、设备诊断信息以及串口数据。密码历史仅在使用密码工具期间保存在内存中，不会持久化到磁盘。

网络工具会从用户设备直接连接到用户选择的本地或远程目标。端口扫描、局域网扫描、Ping 和路由追踪仅在用户主动操作后运行，连接不会经过开发者运营的服务器。用户有责任仅扫描其有权测试的系统和网络。

相机、照片图库、运动传感器、本地网络和 USB 权限，仅在对应功能需要且操作系统支持时请求。二维码扫描与生成、文档导出、运动诊断、局域网发现和串口通信仅在用户主动操作后运行。串口数据只在用户设备与所连接硬件之间传输，不会上传至开发者运营的服务器。

购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。UToolkit 只读取 Apple 提供的商品和权益状态以解锁 Pro 功能，不会接收或保存支付卡信息。Apple 对 App Store 购买和诊断数据的处理受 Apple 隐私政策、用户设备设置及 App Store 设置约束。

Apple 可能依据用户的设备诊断设置向开发者提供崩溃或性能报告。开发者只会将这些报告用于诊断和改进 UToolkit，并且仅在实现该目的所需的期限内保留。

如有隐私问题或删除请求，请在 [UToolKit GitHub 仓库](https://github.com/lslqtz/UToolKit/issues)提交 Issue。请勿在公开 Issue 中包含密码、加密密钥、串口日志、文档或其他敏感内容。
